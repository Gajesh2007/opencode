Send a message to a teammate in your agent team.

Use this to coordinate with other agents working alongside you: hand off a finding, ask a question, or report progress. Local collaboration messages may be up to 20,000 characters and require the recipient to have started a user turn. Messages are queued in the recipient's inbox; they read them with the `inbox` tool. Address teammates by their name (see your team roster). Only available when you are part of a team.
